## Online Therapy Is Now Mainstream in India — And It Actually Works

Three years ago, the idea of talking to a therapist over video call felt awkward, second-rate, or something for people in bigger cities. Today, online therapy is how millions of Indians access mental health support for the first time.

The numbers back this up. A 2024 survey by the Indian Journal of Psychiatry found that **online therapy showed equivalent outcomes to in-person therapy for common mental health conditions** including depression, anxiety, and stress. Dropout rates were lower. Satisfaction scores were higher.

This guide covers everything you need to know to start online therapy in India — whether you're considering it for the first time or trying to figure out whether your current approach is working.

## What Is Online Therapy?

Online therapy (also called e-therapy, teletherapy, or online counselling) is a licensed mental health session conducted remotely — over video call, voice call, or text chat — with a certified therapist, counsellor, or psychologist.

**What it is:**
- A 45–60 minute structured session with a licensed professional
- Evidence-based treatments: CBT, DBT, EMDR, psychodynamic therapy
- Legally the same as in-person therapy in terms of clinical standards

**What it is not:**
- A chatbot or AI conversation (that's something different — more on this later)
- A substitute for crisis care or psychiatric medication management
- Just venting — good therapy is structured, goal-directed, and measurable

## How Does Online Therapy Actually Work?

The mechanics are simpler than most people expect.

**Step 1: Choose a platform or therapist**
You either book through a platform (like ELMA Experts, YourDOST, or iCall) or find a therapist independently and connect via Zoom or Google Meet.

**Step 2: Intake assessment**
Your first session is usually an intake — the therapist understands your situation, goals, and history. Expect questions about sleep, relationships, work, childhood, and what prompted you to seek help. This is not gatekeeping; it helps them tailor the approach.

**Step 3: Regular sessions**
Most people do weekly sessions, 45–60 minutes each. The therapist uses structured techniques (not just listening) and gives you exercises or frameworks to apply between sessions.

**Step 4: Track your progress**
Good therapy has measurable outcomes. After 4–6 sessions, you and your therapist should be reviewing whether the approach is working.

## Is Online Therapy as Effective as In-Person Therapy?

Yes — for most conditions and most people.

A meta-analysis published in *World Psychiatry* (2022) reviewed 452 studies and found that **online CBT was as effective as in-person CBT** for anxiety, depression, and PTSD. A separate review in *JMIR Mental Health* found no significant difference in therapeutic alliance (the quality of relationship between therapist and client) between video and in-person sessions after the first two sessions.

**Conditions where online therapy works well:**
- Generalised anxiety disorder (GAD)
- Depression (mild to moderate)
- Stress and burnout
- Relationship difficulties
- Social anxiety
- Grief and loss
- Imposter syndrome
- Work-related mental health issues

**Conditions that may need in-person or specialised care:**
- Severe depression with suicidal ideation
- Active psychosis
- Eating disorders requiring medical monitoring
- Trauma requiring somatic (body-based) therapy
- Situations requiring psychiatric medication assessment

When in doubt, a first session online can help a therapist tell you if in-person care is more appropriate.

## How to Choose an Online Therapist in India

Not all therapists are equal, and not all platforms have the same vetting standards. Here's what to look for.

### Credentials to Verify

In India, legitimate mental health professionals have one of these qualifications:
- **M.A./M.Sc. Psychology** (minimum for counselling)
- **M.Phil. Clinical Psychology** (RCI-regulated — the gold standard for clinical work)
- **Ph.D. Psychology** (research and clinical)
- **Psychiatrist** (MBBS + MD Psychiatry — for medication)
- **REBT/CBT certification** from accredited bodies (acceptable for focused techniques)

**Red flag:** Anyone claiming to be a "therapist" or "counsellor" without specifying their qualification. In India, these terms are not legally protected — anyone can use them.

### Questions to Ask Before Booking

1. What is your qualification and years of experience?
2. What therapeutic modalities do you use?
3. Have you worked with clients with [your specific issue] before?
4. How do you measure progress in therapy?
5. What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

## What Happens in Your First Online Therapy Session?

First sessions are almost always intake/assessment sessions. Knowing this reduces anxiety about what to expect.

**Typical first session structure (45 minutes):**

- **0–10 min:** Introductions, confidentiality, and consent explanation
- **10–25 min:** Your story — what's bringing you to therapy now
- **25–35 min:** Background — family, relationships, physical health, sleep, substance use (therapists need the full picture to formulate effectively)
- **35–40 min:** Goal-setting — what do you want to be different after therapy?
- **40–45 min:** Next steps — frequency, approach, homework (if applicable)

**What you don't need to do:**
- Have everything figured out
- Know your "diagnosis"
- Have a crisis — you can start therapy because you want to grow, not just because you're breaking down

**What helps:**
- A quiet space where you feel safe to speak freely
- Headphones if you're in a shared home
- A brief note of the key things you want to mention (important things get forgotten in the moment)

## Common Concerns About Online Therapy in India

**"Is it confidential?"**
Yes — licensed therapists are bound by confidentiality rules regardless of the medium. Legitimate platforms use encrypted video and do not record sessions without consent. ELMA Experts uses end-to-end encrypted sessions.

**"What if my family sees I'm in therapy?"**
Use headphones. Most people do therapy from their cars, office, or a private room. Video sessions don't require anyone to know you're doing them.

**"Is online therapy for serious problems or just general stress?"**
Both. CBT delivered online works for clinical depression and anxiety disorder, not just mild stress. The severity of your problem doesn't make online therapy less appropriate — it makes good therapist vetting more important.

**"What if I don't connect with the therapist?"**
This is normal. Therapeutic fit matters enormously. Most platforms allow you to switch therapists. Don't assume one bad match means therapy won't work for you.

**"Will my employer find out?"**
No. Therapists cannot disclose information to employers without your explicit consent.

## How to Know If Online Therapy Is Working

After 6–8 sessions, ask yourself these questions:

1. Do I understand my own patterns better than I did before?
2. Have any of the specific symptoms I came in with reduced?
3. Do I have practical tools I use between sessions?
4. Is there a shared understanding with my therapist of what we're working toward?
5. Am I comfortable being honest with my therapist?

If most of these are "no" after 8 sessions, it may be worth discussing a different approach or a different therapist — not giving up on therapy altogether.
