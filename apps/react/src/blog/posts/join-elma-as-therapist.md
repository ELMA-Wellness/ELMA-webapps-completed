## How ELMA Experts Works

ELMA is India's AI emotional companion app — used by thousands of people daily to track their emotions, journal privately, talk to an AI, and understand their emotional patterns.

When an ELMA user's patterns suggest they would benefit from professional support, the app connects them to ELMA Experts. These are not casual browsers or reluctant participants — they are users who have already:

- Used ELMA for an average of 2–4 weeks before booking
- Tracked their emotional patterns across multiple mood check-ins
- Processed feelings through AI conversation and journalling
- Self-identified the patterns they want help with
- Actively decided to seek professional support

This is the difference between a cold referral and a warm, motivated client. ELMA clients arrive ready to do the work.

## What You Receive as an ELMA Expert

### 1. Mood Data Context (With Client Consent)

When a client shares their ELMA data with you, you receive their Mood Curve — an emotional arc showing their patterns over the past weeks. This includes:
- Which emotional states they experience most frequently
- Which days and situations consistently affect their mood
- Energy level patterns across the day and week
- Journal entry summaries (processed, not raw diary content)

A typical first session without this data requires 30–40 minutes of information gathering before any therapeutic work begins. With ELMA data, you start session one at depth.

### 2. A Client Who Has Already Done the Warm-Up

ELMA clients have already named their emotions, reflected on patterns, and processed surface-level content through AI conversation. They arrive emotionally literate — able to describe what they're feeling with specificity rather than "I just feel bad."

For you, this means sessions that feel productive from minute one. And for clients, it means therapy that feels valuable from the start — which dramatically reduces early dropout.

### 3. Better Session Retention

Industry standard early therapy dropout rates are 20–40% within the first 3–4 sessions. ELMA Experts consistently see lower dropout because:
- Clients are pre-committed (they've already been doing emotional work)
- First sessions are immediately therapeutic (not administrative)
- The AI provides between-session support so clients don't feel unsupported between appointments
- Progress is visible through the Mood Curve, which clients can see improving

For you, this means a more stable caseload and fewer gaps in your schedule from clients who drop out before reaching full engagement.

### 4. Reduced Between-Session Burden

One of the most draining aspects of online practice is managing between-session client contact: anxious messages, brief check-ins, requests for reassurance. ELMA handles this layer. Your clients message ELMA, not you, when they need between-session support. Your professional boundaries are structurally protected, not just verbally stated.

### 5. A Professional Platform That Respects Your Expertise

ELMA Experts operates with a clear professional philosophy: you are the clinical expert, and the platform supports your work. We do not:
- Set rigid session scripts or constrain your clinical approach
- Require you to use specific proprietary methodologies
- Treat you as a commodity by lowering rates below sustainable levels
- Create excessive administrative overhead through onerous documentation requirements

You bring your clinical training and judgment. We bring motivated clients, data context, and a support infrastructure.

## What ELMA Experts Is Not

To set expectations correctly:

**It is not a massive-volume marketplace.** ELMA prioritises client quality and therapist matching over maximum throughput. You will receive a manageable number of well-qualified referrals, not an overwhelming flood of low-quality leads.

**It is not a replacement for your private practice.** ELMA Experts works best as part of a diversified practice portfolio: a platform component providing consistent volume alongside your private caseload.

**It is not passive income.** You earn per session. ELMA provides the client pipeline; you do the clinical work.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many sessions can I expect per week?**
This varies by your availability, specialisation, and how proactively you engage with the platform. Most experts receive 5–15 referrals per week when fully available.

**What are the income arrangements?**
Session rates are set in INR and vary by expert experience level. Rates are shared transparently during onboarding. There are no hidden commission structures.

**Do I need to use ELMA myself to understand my clients' data?**
You'll receive a brief onboarding session explaining how to read ELMA client data. You don't need to be a daily ELMA user — but you'll understand the data with one orientation session.

**What if I don't click with a referred client?**
You are not required to continue with any client where there is a mismatch. Professional fit matters, and ELMA facilitates smooth transitions.

**Can I see clients internationally?**
ELMA is used globally. International client referrals are possible — you should be comfortable with the jurisdictional considerations of online therapy and disclose your country of license clearly.

**How long does onboarding take?**
Application: 5 minutes. Credential verification: 2–5 business days. Onboarding session: 45 minutes. You can be active and receiving referrals within one week.
